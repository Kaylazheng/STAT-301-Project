**Source**: The dataset used in this study is obtained from the UCI Machine Learning Repository.

**Number of observations**: 6,497 (1,599 red, 4,898 white)

**Variables**: The physicochemical properties (such as acidity, sugar, chlorides, sulfur dioxide, density, pH, sulphates, and alcohol) were collected through standard laboratory analytical methods, and the quality scores were produced by human wine experts through sensory blind tests.

**Data Completeness**: This dataset contains no missing values, allowing all 6,497 observations to be utilized directly in our models.

**Representativeness**: The dataset is strictly restricted to Vinho Verde wine samples from the north of Portugal. The original documentation does not provide details on the sampling mechanism. Therefore, our findings may not be fully representative of or generalizable to other wine varieties, regions, or global markets.
